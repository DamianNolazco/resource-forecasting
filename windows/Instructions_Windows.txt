RESOURCE PLANNER — WINDOWS EXE

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a writable folder, such as Documents\ResourcePlanner.
2. Keep Resource_Planner.exe and Resource_Planning_Database.xlsx together.
3. Close the workbook in Excel, then double-click Resource_Planner.exe.
4. Your browser opens the app at http://127.0.0.1:8765. Keep its console open.

Python, Node.js and pip are not required. The app works offline.
Changes save automatically to Excel. Wait for "Saved to Excel" before stopping.
Press Ctrl+C in the console to stop the app. Closing the browser does not stop it.

The workbook contains sample data. To transfer your existing plan, use Export
at https://resources.nolazcolab.site, then Import in this local app.

Before every save, the previous workbook is copied to backups/ beside it.
To edit Excel directly: stop the app, edit/save/close Excel, then restart.
Keep sheet names and row 4 headers unchanged; data begins on row 5.
See the Guide sheet for IDs, dates, allocation percentages and department targets.
Planning dates are January 2026 through December 2027.

If saving fails, Export a JSON backup before closing or reloading the tab.
Close Excel and Retry save for an open-workbook error. If the workbook changed
externally, preserve your edits with Export, then Reload Excel and review it.
Use one app and one editing tab per workbook. There is no online synchronization.

To restore: stop the app, preserve the current workbook, copy a chosen backup
into the main folder and name it Resource_Planning_Database.xlsx. Restart.
Backups are retained until you delete them.

Advanced commands in a terminal opened in this folder:
  .\Resource_Planner.exe --check
  .\Resource_Planner.exe --port 8766
  .\Resource_Planner.exe --workbook "C:\Path\My_Database.xlsx"

Target: 64-bit Intel/AMD Windows. No administrator access required.
This build is unsigned; Windows or company security policy may flag it.
On a managed work computer, follow your IT team's approval process.
The app still uses your browser for its interface; the executable runs the
local server and includes the Python runtime.
